A make.fun entry: two lines a writer sends back to their past self.

Line one warns about one specific moment — signing a contract at the kitchen
table at midnight under a false deadline. Line two traces what it cost:
two years of night shifts for an offer that was never going anywhere.

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.
